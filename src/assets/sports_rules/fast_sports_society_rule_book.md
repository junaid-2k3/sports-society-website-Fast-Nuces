## General Rules

### Fair Play & Conduct
* All matches must be played fairly and in the true spirit of sportsmanship.
* The umpire's/referee's decision is final and cannot be challenged.
* No abusive language, arguments, or unsportsmanlike behavior will be tolerated.
* Any misconduct can lead to disqualification of the player or entire team.
* All participants must maintain respect towards fellow players, referees, coordinators, and volunteers.

### Team & Player Responsibilities
* Each team must submit a full squad list before the tournament starts.
* Team captains are responsible for the behavior and punctuality of their teams.
* Players must wear proper sports attire (team jerseys if possible).
* Participants can register only once per category (e.g., one badminton singles, one doubles).

### Match and Timing Rules
* Teams must report 15 minutes before their scheduled match.
* Walkovers will be awarded at the discretion of tournament management if teams are late.
* Identification (original CNIC or student ID) must be carried at all times and shown upon request.

### Equipment and Game Standards
* Only official tournament equipment (balls, etc.) will be used.
* Standard rules will apply with minor sport-specific adjustments.

### Miscellaneous
* The tournament committee reserves the right to amend rules if necessary.
* The society reserves the right to change rules at any time during the event if necessary.
* **Withdrawal after registration is not allowed.**
* By registering, participants consent to photography and videography during the event for official use.

## Individual Game Rules

### Cricket (Male)
* **Dates:** 5 Oct – 8 Oct
* **Players:** 9 Players (8 playing, 1 substitute)
* **Registration:** 5,500 PKR

#### Game Specific Rules
* Each team must submit a full squad list before the tournament starts.
* Matches will be played with a Tennis Ball (unless specified otherwise).
* 5 overs per side in group stages; may increase to 6 to 8 overs in semifinals/finals.
* Two bowlers can bowl 2 overs in a 5-over match.
* Overthrows will be counted.
* Bouncers above shoulder height will be called wide.
* Direct underarm bowling is not allowed; bowling must have proper action.
* **Wide ball:** +1 run and extra delivery.
* **In case of a tie:**
  * A Super Over will decide the winner.
  * If still tied, most boundaries scored will decide.

#### Equipment
* Only official tournament tennis balls will be used.

### Futsal (Male)
* **Dates:** 5 Oct – 8 Oct
* **Players:** 8 Players (5 playing, 3 substitutes)
* **Registration:** 4,500 PKR

#### Game Specific Rules
* **Match duration:** 2 halves of 15 minutes (stopped clock).
* **Half-time break:** 5 minutes.
* **Timeouts:** 1 per team per half (duration: 1 minute).
* **Extra Time (if needed):** 2 halves of 3 minutes each.
* **Tiebreaker (after extra time):** Penalty shootout (5 kicks each, then sudden death if still tied).
* **Team composition:** 8 players total (5 on the field, 3 substitutes). Unlimited substitutions allowed through the substitution zone.
* Kick-ins instead of throw-ins (must be taken within 4 seconds).
* The goalkeeper must throw the ball on goal clearance (no kicking).
* Corner kicks and free kicks must be taken within 4 seconds.
* Penalty mark at 6 meters; second penalty mark at 10 meters (after 5 accumulated fouls).
* Sliding tackles allowed only if safe and non-aggressive.
* **Goalkeeper rules:**
  * May only touch the ball once per team possession.
  * Must release the ball within 4 seconds.
  * Cannot use hands on back-passes or kick-ins from teammates.
* **Fouls and Discipline:**
  * After 5 accumulated fouls, opponents get a direct free kick from 10 meters.
  * **Yellow Card:** Warning/caution to players.
  * **Red Card:** Player sent off; team plays short for 2 minutes or until opponent scores.

### Table Tennis (Male / Female)
* **Dates:** 5 Oct – 8 Oct
* **Players:** Single
* **Registration:** Singles: 600 PKR

#### Game Specific Rules
* Matches will follow the International Table Tennis Federation rules.
* Matches before the semi-finals will be played as the best of 3 games.
* Matches from the semi-finals onwards will be played as the best of 5 games.
* Each game will be played with 11 points. A player must win by at least 2 points.
* Players must switch sides after each game.
* In doubles, players must alternate shots during a rally.
* A toss will decide which player/team serves first.
* Service must be behind the end line and above the table surface.
* If a player touches the table with their free hand during a rally, they lose the point.
* If a player moves the table from its original position during a rally, they lose the point.
* Any misconduct, including abusive language or unsporting behavior, may result in disqualification.
* Players must report 15 minutes prior to their scheduled match time.
* No outside coaching is allowed during matches.

#### Equipment
* Standard table tennis balls and rackets must be used.
* Players are encouraged to bring their own rackets.

### Badminton (Male / Female)
* **Dates:** 5 Oct – 8 Oct
* **Players:** Single / Double
* **Registration:**
  * **Singles:** 500 PKR (Male) / 300 PKR (Female)
  * **Doubles:** 1,000 PKR (Male) / 600 PKR (Female)

#### Game Specific Rules
* **Qualifying Matches:** Knockout matches (single set to 21 points).
* **Simple Matches (for qualified players):** Knockout matches (single set to 21 points).
* **Semi-Finals and Finals:** Best of 3 sets (each set to 21 points).
* A match is usually played to 21 points per set.
* At 20 all, the side which gains a 2-point lead first wins the set.
* At 29 all, the side scoring the 30th point wins the set.
* The side winning a set serves first in the next set.
* Players must switch sides after each set.
* In a third set (if played), players must switch sides when one side reaches 11 points.

#### Equipment
* Shuttlecock will be provided by the organizers.
* Players must use standard badminton rackets.

### Volleyball (Male)

* **Registration:** 3,500 PKR

#### Game Specific Rules
* **Teams:** Two teams with six players each.

* **Sets:** Best two out of three or best three out of five sets.
* **Set scoring:** 25 points with a minimum lead of two points.

### Chess (Male / Female)
* **Dates:** 5 Oct – 8 Oct
* **Players:** Individual
* **Registration:** 500 PKR (Male/Female)

#### Game Specific Rules
* The tournament will follow FIDE (International Chess Federation) rules.
* Time control will be 10 minutes + 2 seconds increment per move.
* Swiss style tournament format, tentatively 5 rounds.
* **Scoring system:**
  * Win: 1 point
  * Draw: 0.5 point
  * Loss: 0 points
* Players must be present 15 minutes before the start of their first round.
* **In case of an illegal move:**
  * The player must stop the clock immediately and call the arbiter.
  * The arbiter will enforce penalties according to FIDE laws (may include time penalties or forfeiture after multiple illegal moves).
* If a player does not show up within 15 minutes of the round start, they forfeit the round.
* No external assistance (coaching, mobile phones, etc.) is allowed during games.
* Unsportsmanlike behavior, abusive language, or disturbing the opponent may result in immediate disqualification.
* The tournament director/arbiter's decisions will be final and binding.

### Ludo (Male / Female)
* **Dates:** 5 Oct – 8 Oct
* **Players:** Double
* **Registration:** Doubles: 1,000 PKR (Male/Female)

#### Game Specific Rules
* Matches will be played 2v2.
* Standard Ludo board and rules will be followed.
* To move a piece out of the home base, a 6 must be rolled.
* Each 6 gives an extra turn; however, three consecutive 6s cause the turn to be forfeited.
* A player can capture an opponent's token by landing on the same square (sending it back to base).
* Safe zones (marked with a star) protect the tokens from being captured.
* The winner is the player/team who successfully brings all four tokens to the finish first.
* To get into the house, the player has to beat at least one piece.
* The opponent player cannot beat two pieces if they are both on each other.
* Time limit may be applied to matches to avoid delays (specifics will be announced on match day).
* Misconduct, unnecessary arguments, or delaying tactics will lead to warnings or disqualification.

### Dodgeball (Male)
* **Dates:** 5 Oct – 8 Oct
* **Players:** 6–12 Players
* **Registration:** 1,000 PKR

#### Game Specific Rules
* **Teams:** Two teams with 6–12 players each.
* Two teams line up on opposite ends of the court, an equal number of balls are placed along the center line.
* On the whistle, both teams approach the center line to collect balls for the opening rush.
* Players throw balls at opponents to hit and eliminate them, while dodging, blocking, or catching balls thrown at them.
* A player is eliminated if they are hit directly by a thrown ball (below the neck) or if a thrown ball they attempted to catch touches the ground first.
* If a player successfully catches a ball thrown by an opponent before it touches the ground, the thrower is eliminated instead, and one eliminated teammate may return to the game.
* Players may block an incoming ball using a ball held in their own hands; if it is knocked loose and touches the ground, the blocking player is out.
* The round continues until all players of one team are eliminated, or the timer runs out. The team with more players remaining wins that round.
* A match is typically played as best-of-three rounds; the team winning two rounds wins the match.
* The type of game will be decided at match time.

### UNO (Male / Female)
* **Dates:** 5 Oct – 8 Oct
* **Players:** 4–6 Players per table
* **Registration:** 200 PKR / per person (Male/Female)

#### Game Specific Rules
* **Format:** Individual game; recommended 4–6 players per table.
* Each player is dealt 7 cards at the start.
* Players must play a card matching the top card by colour, number, or symbol.
* If a player cannot play, they must draw one card. If playable, it may be played immediately.
* Players must call **"UNO"** when they are left with one card.
* If a player forgets to call UNO and is caught before the next player starts, they must draw 2 penalty cards.
* **Skip:** Next player loses their turn.
* **Reverse:** Changes the direction of play.
* **Draw Two (+2):** Next player draws 2 cards and loses their turn.
* **Wild:** Player chooses the colour to continue.
* **Wild Draw Four (+4):** Player chooses the colour; next player draws 4 cards and loses their turn. It can only be played when the player has no card matching the current colour.
* **No stacking:** +2 and +4 cards cannot be stacked.
* **No house rules:** Jump-in, 7-0, and other unofficial rules are not allowed.
* Players must not coach or interfere with another player's turn.
* Playing an illegal card results in taking the card back and drawing 2 penalty cards.
* **Winner:** The first player to play their final card wins the game.
* If the final card is +2 or +4, the next player must take the applicable cards before the game is closed.

### Mafia Wars (Male / Female)
* **Dates:** 5 Oct – 8 Oct
* **Players:** 10–12 players per game
* **Registration:** 100 PKR / per person (Male/Female)

#### Game Specific Rules
* **Format:** 12 players per game + 1 Host. Individual participation; no teams.
* Each player receives a secret role and must keep it confidential.
* The game alternates between Night and Day phases.
* **Night Phase:** Mafia selects one player to eliminate, while the Doctor can protect one player and the Detective can investigate one player. Psycho Civilian can use their special ability once per game.
* **Day Phase:** Players discuss and vote to eliminate one suspected player. Every living player gets one vote. In case of a tie, a revote is held; if the tie remains, no player is eliminated.
* **Win Conditions:**
  * **Mafia wins** when the number of Mafia members equals or exceeds the remaining non-Mafia players.
  * **Town wins** when both Mafia members are eliminated.
  * **Jester wins** individually if voted out by the Town during the Day.
* Players must not reveal or show their role cards unless required by the Host.
* The Host manages all phases, actions, votes, and disputes, and their decision is final.

### Sequence Game (Male / Female)
* **Dates:** 5 Oct – 8 Oct
* **Players:** Team or Individual Game (2–12 Players)
* **Registration:** 200 PKR / per person (Male/Female)

#### Game Specific Rules
* **Goal:** Get 5 chips in a row (horizontal, vertical, diagonal) = a Sequence.
* **Win Condition:**
  * 2 players $\rightarrow$ 2 Sequences
  * 3 players $\rightarrow$ 1 Sequence
  * Teams $\rightarrow$ 2 Sequences (2 teams) or 1 Sequence (3 teams)
* **Setup:** Deal cards (7 for 2p, 6 for 3–4p, 5 for 6p, 4 for 8–9p); rest is draw deck.
* **Turn:** Play a card $\rightarrow$ place matching chip on board $\rightarrow$ draw new card (must draw before next turn or lose that draw).
* **Jacks (Wilds):**
  * **Two-eyed Jack:** Place chip anywhere.
  * **One-eyed Jack:** Remove an opponent's chip (not from a completed Sequence).
* **Corners:** Free for everyone; only need 4 chips + corner to complete a Sequence.
* **Dead card:** If both matching board spaces are filled, discard it on your turn and draw a replacement.
* **Team play:** No coaching/signaling (violation costs a card from hand).
* **Overlap:** One chip can be shared between two Sequences, but the second needs 4 new chips in a different direction.

### Tug of War (Male)
* **Dates:** 5 Oct – 8 Oct
* **Players:** Team Game (6 Players + 2 Substitutes)
* **Registration:** 2,000 PKR

#### Game Specific Rules
* **Starting Position:** Rope centered over midline; hold with both hands (no wrapping around body/hands).
* **Victory Condition:** Win by pulling opponent past centerline; falling or releasing rope = automatic loss of that round.
* **Footwear & Attire:** Sports shoes required (no barefoot/spikes); gloves allowed if organizer-approved.
* **Fouls & Disqualifications:** No sitting/lying down while pulling; no suddenly letting go of rope; unsportsmanlike behavior (pushing, shouting in ear, obstructing) $\rightarrow$ immediate disqualification.
